Privacy policy

Updated 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Talent Press (we, us) processes personal data about visitors to talent-press.com and people who send us enquiries. We aim to explain our practices clearly and collect only the data we need.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Elizaveta Zamotaeva (sole proprietor trading as Talent Press). Address: Stralauer Allee 13A, 10245 Berlin, Germany. For questions about personal data, email visa.talent.press@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Form and quiz data: name, phone number linked to WhatsApp or Telegram, Telegram username, preferred contact time and quiz answers, such as country, professional field, experience, city, status and budget.
  • Information you provide directly: messages, emails and consultation information, including documents and details of your achievements if you choose to share them.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, visit times and referral source. Cookies and similar technologies may collect this data; see our Cookie Policy.

We do not request passport details, payment-card data, health information, nationality or political views through the website. Please do not submit this information through the forms.

4. Who may receive your data

We do not sell or rent your data. Access may be given to services and contractors needed for our work: website hosting and security, enquiry and messaging services such as Telegram, WhatsApp and email, CRM providers, web and advertising analytics services following cookie consent, and specialists you ask to involve in your case, such as your attorney. They process data on our behalf or at your request and must protect it. We may also disclose data in response to a valid request from public authorities.

5. International transfers

If processing requires a transfer outside the European Economic Area, we use the legal bases and safeguards required by law.

6. Retention

Enquiry data is retained for no longer than 3 years after the last contact if you do not become a client. Client data is retained during the contract and for any legally required periods. Analytics retention depends on the relevant cookies and service. You may request earlier deletion as described in section 8.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption and restrict access by staff and contractors. We aim to use services with clear security practices. No system is completely secure. Where required by law, we will notify affected people and the relevant regulator of a data incident within the applicable deadlines.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may be able to access your data, obtain a copy, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing, opt out of marketing and complain to a supervisory authority. This may be Roskomnadzor in Russia or the relevant data-protection authority in the EU or UK.

For residents of California and other U.S. states: You may have rights to information about the data collected, deletion and correction. We do not sell personal data or disclose it for cross-context behavioural advertising without your consent, and we do not discriminate against people for exercising their rights.

To exercise your rights, write to visa.talent.press@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by law, generally within 30 days, or within 10 working days for requests from Russia.

9. Children

The website and services are intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can address the request and delete it where appropriate.

10. Links to other websites

The website may link to third-party resources, such as government websites, messaging services and social networks. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The revised version takes effect when published on this page, with its version date shown above. We will provide additional notice on the website of material changes.
