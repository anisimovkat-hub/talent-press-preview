About the permit

A multi-year residence permit for professionals

The talent permit allows eligible holders to live and work in France under the conditions of their category. Categories include certain professionals, researchers, artists and entrepreneurs.

🗓️ Up to 4 years

A multi-year residence permit; the duration depends on the category and decision.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family

A spouse and minor children may use the accompanying-family procedure, subject to the applicable requirements.

💻 Online applications

Many applications use ANEF and are submitted by you or your attorney.

🧭 The relevant category

Your immigration attorney advises on the appropriate category.

Common mistakes

Issues that weaken the evidence and how to address them

📢

Advertising presented as independent recognition

Promotional and sponsored material should not be treated as independent recognition without examining the evidence and applicable requirements.

🧩

Disconnected evidence

Awards, publications and letters should form a clear, consistent record of your professional work.

🔎

Evidence that is hard to verify

Links, dates and sources should be easy to verify. Inaccuracies can undermine confidence in the record.

🌐

Translations and presentation

Foreign-language documents generally need translation. Confirm the required format with your attorney and the prefecture.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as Passeport Talent?

The current name is the multi-year talent residence permit, carte de séjour pluriannuelle « talent ». Passeport Talent is the former name and is still commonly used.

How long is the permit valid?

The talent residence permit can be valid for up to 4 years. The precise duration depends on the category and the relevant decision.

What documents are required?

The documents depend on the category and the instructions of the prefecture or consulate. We prepare evidence of your recognition and professional contribution; the complete application requirements should be agreed with your attorney.

Can my family accompany me?

The accompanying-family procedure covers a spouse and minor children, subject to the applicable conditions. Confirm residence-document requirements and the rules when a child turns 18 with your attorney and service-public.gouv.fr.

How is an application submitted?

Many talent residence permit applications are submitted online through ANEF. You or your attorney handle the application; we prepare supporting case materials.

Do I need to speak French?

Language requirements depend on the relevant status and current rules. Confirm the requirements that apply to your case with your attorney.

Are you a law firm?

No. We prepare evidence and case materials and work alongside your attorney. We do not provide legal services.

Can the rules change?

Yes. Conditions, thresholds and procedures are updated. Check service-public.gouv.fr and consult your attorney before applying.
