Understanding the criterion

What you need to know

EB-1A and O-1A include criteria concerning nationally or internationally recognised prizes or awards for excellence in the relevant field. The award's standing and selection process need documentary support.

✅ What may qualify

  • Industry awards with independent judges and a meaningful selection process
  • Nationally or internationally recognised awards for excellence
  • Awards with public selection criteria and information about organisers

⚠️ What may fall short

  • Awards given to all participants without selection
  • Participation certificates
  • Prizes with no verifiable information about organisers or judges
  • Awards outside your professional field

Will any competition qualify?

No. Its standing, selection process and judges matter. A prize without a meaningful selection process is unlikely to establish the relevant recognition.

How do I document a competition's significance?

Include information about the organisers, selection criteria, judges, number of participants and coverage in professional publications.
